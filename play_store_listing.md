Welcome to Rental, your ultimate property-finding companion! Whether you are a student looking for a cozy PG, a professional searching for an apartment, or a family wanting a spacious independent house, Rental connects you directly with the best listings in your city.

**Key Features:**

📍 **Smart Radius Search**
Instantly see properties available right around you. Our smart feed dynamically searches within a 5km radius, automatically expanding if you want to explore further!

🏢 **Homes & PGs**
Switch seamlessly between finding a full home/apartment or finding a PG/Co-living space. Each property type shows the details that matter to you (like AC, food availability, and curfew rules for PGs, or furnishing status for houses).

🗺️ **Interactive Maps**
Don't just browse lists—see exactly where the properties are located on our interactive, beautifully integrated map view. 

📸 **Beautiful Details**
View high-quality, watermarked photos and comprehensive details of every property. Know exactly what you're getting before you even visit.

📞 **Direct Contact**
Found a place you love? Tap to instantly call the owner or property manager. No middlemen, no hidden fees!

**For Property Owners & Admins:**
Posting a property has never been easier. Upload your photos, fill out the amenities, and your listing instantly goes live to thousands of searchers in your area. Our built-in image processor automatically secures your uploaded photos with a watermark.

Download Rental today and find your next perfect home effortlessly!
