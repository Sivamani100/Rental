# Child Safety Standards and CSAE Prevention Policy

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026
**App Name:** Rental - Find Homes & PGs
**Developer:** ArkioLabs
**Designated Contact Email:** mallipurapusiva@gmail.com

At ArkioLabs, we are deeply committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and secure environment for all users of the **Rental** app. We have a **strict, zero-tolerance policy** against any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

As a platform that allows users to post property and PG listings, we mandate strict adherence to child safety laws. Any user found violating these policies will face immediate and permanent bans, and will be reported to the relevant regional, national, and international authorities.

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy on CSAM and CSAE
We strictly prohibit any content, behavior, or activity on our platform that promotes, distributes, or creates Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or encourages Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).
- Users may not upload, share, or link to any imagery, text, or multimedia depicting or promoting the exploitation of minors.
- Users may not use our messaging, listing, or contact features to groom, solicit, or exploit children in any manner.

## 2. Content Moderation and Removal
We actively monitor and review reports regarding inappropriate content on our platform.
- If any CSAM or CSAE content is detected or reported, it will be **immediately removed** from public visibility and preserved securely for law enforcement review.
- Accounts associated with the distribution or creation of such material will be **permanently disabled**.

## 3. Reporting Mechanisms
We provide robust mechanisms for users to report any safety concerns or violations directly to our moderation team:
- **In-App Reporting:** Users can use the "Report" feature available on every property listing to flag inappropriate content or behavior.
- **Direct Email Reporting:** Users, parents, or law enforcement officials can directly report suspected child safety violations or CSAM to our designated safety team at **mallipurapusiva@gmail.com**. We prioritize these reports and review them immediately.

## 4. Law Enforcement and Authority Cooperation
We comply with all relevant child safety laws globally and within our operating regions. 
- We are committed to reporting any instances of CSAM or CSAE to the appropriate regional and national authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or local cybercrime units.
- We will fully cooperate with law enforcement investigations, including preserving and providing relevant user data, IP logs, and communications as required by law.

## 5. Point of Contact
For any concerns, questions, or immediate reports regarding child safety, compliance, and our CSAM prevention practices, please contact our designated safety point of contact:

**Email:** mallipurapusiva@gmail.com
